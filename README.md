# HyperOx mobile

HyperOx mobile is an Android app (Flutter) that lets you watch and steer the AI coding agents running in your HyperOx terminals from your phone: read the inbox, approve or deny agent requests, send input, and open a live full-colour terminal.

## Two ways to connect

- **HyperOx account**: sign in once and your computers appear on the phone. Traffic goes through an end-to-end encrypted relay; the relay never sees terminal content. Push alerts when an agent needs you.
- **Direct SSH / Tailscale**: no account needed. The phone connects straight to `h0x-core` over SSH, or over Tailscale SSH.
